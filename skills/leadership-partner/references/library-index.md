# Approach library: 19 routes

Read [theory-selection.md](theory-selection.md) first. Select a route only after the episode and possible causes have been investigated. Then read its [behavioural route](competency-routes.md) and the relevant [source record](library.md). A source is checked only for the narrow claim stated there; the library does not validate the full book, branded technique, or AI activity. Candidate works require a fresh passage check before substantive attribution. PROD artifacts are our adaptations, not methods proved by the cited source.
