# Bounded approach records

This shared ledger inherits narrow source claims from the Russian v0.10.0-rc2 library checked on 2026-10-06. Translation and packaging did not independently reopen every original. “Checked” means only that the prior review recorded a relevant accessible passage or abstract for the stated claim. It does **not** mean the whole work was read, the approach was validated, or this AI adaptation works. Some mirrors and OCR were limited. Use [theory-selection.md](theory-selection.md) when a claim needs fresh confirmation or the context differs. PROD examples and worksheets are authored adaptations.

For author, year, exact version, access level, sections actually read in the prior review, companion studies, and unverified search candidates, consult [source provenance](source-provenance.json). Look up the route entry ID in `route_sources`, then its record IDs. These historical access records do not claim fresh reading in the current conversation. Candidate metadata can itself require bibliographic verification. The catalog remains open: verify and use a better-fitting external approach when appropriate.

## C01

### C01-SIMON

**Source:** [A Behavioral Model of Rational Choice](https://cooperative-individualism.org/simon-herbert_a-behavioral-model-of-rational-choice-1955-feb.pdf).

**Checked claim:** Bounded search and an acceptable threshold describe choice without computing a global optimum.

**Boundary:** A theory of choice, not a validated decision worksheet; mandatory safety thresholds remain mandatory.

### C01-KK

**Source:** [Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/kahneman-klein-2009.pdf).

**Checked claim:** Expert intuition is more credible where cues are sufficiently regular and timely feedback enables learning.

**Boundary:** Confidence alone is no test of expertise; do not use this as a rating of a person.

### C01-ACCOUNT

**Source:** [The Effect of Outcome vs. Process Accountability-Focus on Performance: A Meta-Analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.795117/full).

**Checked claim:** Across nine laboratory experiments, the review found no significant overall advantage of process over outcome accountability, with high heterogeneity.

**Boundary:** No universal rule that one accountability focus improves management decisions.

## C02

### C02-SDT

**Checked claim:** Self-determination theory distinguishes autonomous from controlled motivation and considers autonomy, competence and relatedness support.

**Boundary:** Do not infer an employee's motivation from one account or treat autonomy as an absence of accountability.

### C02-LAS

**Source:** [Leader Autonomy Support in the Workplace: A Meta-analytic Review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11031-018-9698-y).

**Checked claim:** Leader autonomy support is associated with favourable work outcomes in the reviewed studies.

**Boundary:** Much of the evidence is cross-sectional; direction and causality remain uncertain.

### C02-SLII

**Source:** [A Situational Approach to Effective Leadership](https://www.blanchard.com/blog/a-situational-approach-to-effective-leadership).

**Checked claim:** The provider describes task-specific competence and commitment, and a practical distinction between direction and support.

**Boundary:** Provider account is not independent proof of matching effectiveness; do not classify a person by chat or merge SLII with older versions.

## C03

### C03-GOAL

**Source:** [New Directions in Goal-Setting Theory](https://rt3nc.org/objects/modules/minimods/smartgoals/old/Locke%20et%20al%20New%20dir%20goal%20setting%2006-article.pdf).

### C03-ROLE

**Checked claim:** Role ambiguity showed a negative association with job performance in the reviewed evidence.

**Boundary:** Associations, often from self-report, do not diagnose an individual's problem.

### C03-MONITOR

**Source:** [Does Monitoring Goal Progress Promote Goal Attainment? A Meta-Analysis of the Experimental Evidence](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/91437/8/3_PDFsam_Does%20monitoring%20goal.pdf).

**Boundary:** Only one of 138 studies was outside health; transfer to management checkpoints is indirect.

## C06

### C06-CONFLICT

**Source:** [The Paradox of Team Conflict Revisited: An Updated Meta-Analysis of the Team Conflict–Team Performance Relationships](https://eprints.lse.ac.uk/129181/1/The_paradox_of_team_conflict_revisited.pdf).

## C07

### C07-TRANSFER

**Source:** [Transfer of Training: A Meta-Analytic Review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235975809_Transfer_of_Training_A_Meta-Analytic_Review).

**Checked claim:** Training transfer relates to the work environment as well as the learner; associations were larger when transfer and its predictors were measured from the same source in the same context.

### C07-TRAIN

**Boundary:** Moderators are not a randomised recipe; no proof of this AI workflow or guaranteed transfer.

## Candidate and excluded uses

The following are candidates or unverified precise attributions, not endorsed methods in this package. Reopen the original relevant passage before using a substantive claim: Vroom–Yetton 1973 decision tree; recognition-primed decision making 1993 original chapter; Hackman–Oldham 1976 job characteristics text; early Hersey–Blanchard writings and later situational-leadership validation studies; precise SLII matching effects; Mento–Steel–Karren goal-setting effect estimate; Kluger–DeNisi 1996 full text (the 1998 author discussion above is narrower); an incompletely verified 2025 difficult-conversations review; Jehn 1995 and De Dreu–Weingart 2003 full conflict methods; formal workplace-mediation evidence; Baldwin–Ford 1988 original; some reflection and team-design works. Bibliography or an abstract does not resolve the missing passage.

SMART, SBI, DESC and NVC are also outside the set of verified author-prescribed methods in this source pass. A neutral task brief or observation sheet may be offered as PROD without attributing it to those brands. Do not merge early Situational Leadership, its later versions, and SLII; the checked C02-SLII item is only a provider description, not independent evidence of matching efficacy. Candidate status is not a claim that an approach is ineffective.
